Knowledge Center

Demystifying financial instruments so you can invest with clarity, conviction, and emotional discipline.

SIP vs Lump Sum Strategy

SIP averages volatility across bull and bear markets with monthly discipline. Lump sum is optimal when substantial cash reserves are available and equities trade at reasonable valuation multiples.

Ideal For: Regular monthly salary earners & entrepreneurs
Large, Mid & Small Cap Equity

Large Caps (Top 100 firms) provide core stability. Mid Caps (101-250) capture emerging sector winners. Small Caps offer aggressive alpha over 7+ year horizons.

Ideal For: 5 to 15+ years long-term compounding goals
Balanced Advantage (BAF) Hybrid

Dynamic asset allocation funds that automatically increase equity exposure during market corrections and shift into debt instruments when market valuations run expensive.

Ideal For: First-time equity investors seeking calm ride
ELSS Tax Saving Funds Tax Saver

Equity Linked Savings Schemes carry the shortest lock-in period (3 years) among all Section 80C options (compared to PPF 15 years), with superior long-term inflation-beating equity potential.

Ideal For: Tax deduction up to ₹1.5 Lakhs under Sec 80C
Liquid & Arbitrage Funds Capital Safety

High liquidity with negligible interest rate risk. Perfect for parking emergency funds or storing capital earmarked for systematic transfer plans (STP) into equity schemes.

Ideal For: Emergency reserves & short-term parking

## Brand & Style

This design system delivers a high-conviction wealth management experience that bridges institutional rigor with vibrant, forward-looking financial momentum. Built around an energetic wealth-growth motif inspired by upward market trajectories and sunrise golden hues, the visual architecture targets affluent individuals, proactive portfolio builders, and advisory clients seeking institutional-grade advisory with contemporary digital fluency.

### Emotional Response & Personality
- **Authoritative & Dynamic:** Uncompromising security and structured clarity paired with an active, optimistic growth narrative.
- **Warm Institutional:** Steers clear of sterile, overly cold banking palettes; embraces warm solar oranges and sunlit golds grounded by obsidian and slate charcoals.
- **Disciplined Precision:** High data density, crisp edge definition, meticulous typographic scale, and purposeful visual signifiers for yields, asset allocations, and advisory performance.

### Aesthetic Movement
The design movement is **Corporate Modern with High-Contrast Energy**. It combines ultra-crisp white floating card surfaces, refined 1px border structures, ambient warm micro-glows, and deep carbon typography with Plus Jakarta Sans to deliver an immediate impression of credibility, wealth expansion, and technical excellence.

## Colors

The color system draws directly from the core visual identity: a sunrise gradient from golden yellow to rich sunset orange, anchored by deep charcoal slate and pristine card backgrounds.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#EA580C` - Solar Orange):** The primary growth driver. Applied to primary calls-to-action, key portfolio metrics, active tabs, progress trajectories, and upward financial milestones. Supported by `#FF7A00` for luminous interactive states and `#C2410C` for pressed/deep states.
- **Secondary (`#FBBF24` - Amber Sun / Wealth Accent):** Used for highlighting premium advisory tiers, yield signals, milestone achievements, and subtle visual badges.
- **Tertiary (`#0F172A` - Midnight Slate):** Provides high-contrast anchor tones for primary headers, navigation headers, statement tables, and executive summaries.
- **Neutral (`#475569` - Slate Neutral):** Powers secondary data labels, subtle divider lines (`#E2E8F0`), muted table backgrounds (`#F8FAFC`), and supporting captions.

### Canvas & Surface Structure
The primary canvas adopts a crystalline, light foundation (`#F8FAFC` to `#FFFFFF`) paired with stark white card containers (`#FFFFFF`), ensuring charts, financial ledgers, and dynamic tickers pop with clarity and zero chromatic fatigue.

## Typography

The typography system relies exclusively on **Plus Jakarta Sans**, offering a clean, contemporary grotesque sans-serif with subtle geometric warmth and high legibility across dense fiscal reports and portfolio dashboards.

### Rules & Hierarchy
- **Numerical Dominance:** Large metric callouts (AUM, portfolio value, alpha gains) utilize `display-lg` and `display-sm` with bold weight (`700` or `800`) and tighter letter tracking (`-0.03em`) for a solid, institutional silhouette.
- **Labels & Micro-Metrics:** All financial micro-indicators, ticker changes, status badges, and column headers leverage `label-md` or `label-sm` set in uppercase or semi-bold with positive tracking to optimize scan speed.
- **Editorial Balance:** Explanatory advisory notes and market sentiment commentaries use `body-lg` or `body-md` in regular weight to prevent visual heaviness against high-contrast figures.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is engineered around a disciplined **12-column fluid grid** for desktop and wide screens, collapsing cleanly into 6 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile.

### Spatial Rhythm
- **Rhythm Multiplier:** Anchored to a strict 8px base rhythm (`space-xs` = 4px, `space-sm` = 8px, `space-md` = 16px, `space-lg` = 24px, `space-xl` = 40px).
- **Responsive Handling:** Desktop interfaces maintain generous margins of `2rem` (32px) and gutters of `1.5rem` (24px) to preserve white space around intensive data modules. Mobile views contract to `1rem` margins and `1rem` gutters, prioritizing full-bleed tabular swipes and card readability.
- **Density Management:** Advisory views allow for compact inline rows (`space-sm` vertical gap) within transaction lists, expanding to `space-lg` and `space-xl` in strategic portfolio overviews and onboarding steps.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a refined pairing of **tonal layering**, **crisp micro-borders**, and **warm ambient shadows**. This prevents muddy overlays and keeps financial interfaces feeling crisp and authoritative.

### Elevation Hierarchy
1. **Canvas Level (0dp):** Crisp light wash (`#F8FAFC`), framing interactive content cleanly.
2. **Card & Surface Containers (1dp):** Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) with a hairline border (`1px solid #F1F5F9`) and an extra-diffused, subtle shadow: `0px 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0px 4px 12px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
3. **Interactive & Hover Cards (2dp):** Elevates on mouse hover with `0px 8px 24px rgba(234, 88, 12, 0.08), 0px 2px 6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`, subtly infusing an orange-amber halo to emphasize investment vitality.
4. **Modals, Drawers & Portfolios Overlays (3dp):** Elevated with a deep structural shadow `0px 20px 40px -8px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.16)` and 1px border (`#E2E8F0`), backdropped by a soft frosted veil (`backdrop-filter: blur(8px); background: rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.4)`).

## Shapes

The design system incorporates a `roundedness: 2` scale (0.5rem base) to balance structural authority with approachable modernity.

### Corner Radius System
- **Micro Elements (Tags, Indicators, Badges):** 0.375rem (`rounded-sm`) for snappy, tabular precision.
- **Form Controls & Buttons:** 0.5rem (`rounded-md`) providing a balanced, tactile touchpoint.
- **Content Cards & Data Modules:** 0.75rem (`rounded-lg`) to 1rem (`rounded-xl`), creating comfortable, distinct containers for asset performance graphs.
- **Pill Badges & Growth Pills:** Full rounded `9999px` strictly reserved for ticker changes (e.g., `+18.4%`), status pills, and user avatars.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid gradient background transitioning from `#FF7A00` to `#EA580C`. White text (`#FFFFFF`), `font-weight: 600`, subtle warm hover glow (`box-shadow: 0 4px 14px rgba(234, 88, 12, 0.35)`).
- **Secondary (Institutional):** Dark slate surface (`#0F172A`) with white text for secondary executive actions (e.g., "Export Statement", "Rebalance Assets").
- **Outline / Ghost:** Hairline border (`1.5px solid #E2E8F0`), dark slate text (`#0F172A`), active states transitioning with warm orange border highlights (`#EA580C`).

### Cards & Financial Tiles
- Structured on pure `#FFFFFF` backgrounds with `1px solid #E2E8F0`.
- Top metrics present an accent line or an upward gradient micro-sparkline.
- Clear separation between card header, analytical content, and action footer using `space-md` internal padding.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, text `#0F172A`.
- Focus state: Border turns `#EA580C` with a 3px matching ring at `rgba(234, 88, 12, 0.15)`.
- Numeric inputs for investment contributions feature bold numeric styling and integrated currency prefix labels (`label-md`).

### Status Badges & Chips
- **Growth / Positive Gain:** Light amber-orange background (`#FFF7ED`), text `#C2410C`, paired with a solid `#EA580C` upward trend arrow.
- **Neutral / Holding:** Light slate (`#F1F5F9`), text `#475569`.
- **Warning / Alert:** Soft amber background (`#FEF3C7`), text `#B45309`.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Unchecked: Neutral slate ring (`#94A3B8`) over `#FFFFFF`.
- Checked: Deep orange background (`#EA580C`) with a crisp white tick or inner dot.

### Wealth-Specific Components
- **Allocation Donut & Sparklines:** Use the vibrant orange-to-gold palette (`#EA580C`, `#F97316`, `#FBBF24`, `#0F172A`, `#334155`).
- **Growth Benchmark Banner:** High-contrast dark charcoal header card (`#0F172A`) with golden-orange metric callouts (`#FBBF24` and `#FF7A00`) symbolizing aggressive compounding and financial security.
